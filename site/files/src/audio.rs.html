//! The player's sounds, as baked by `tools/bake_sounds.py`: per clip, the//! frames its sound events fire on, and per sound, the game's choice of what//! to play (one of several recordings, several at once), each at the level and//! pitch its Wwise mix gives it, varied a little every time as the game does.//!
